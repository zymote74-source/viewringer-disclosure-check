# Banned & caution wording (English, free edition — 35 entries)

Compare against this list when checking. Catch variants (relieves/relieving), spellings, and synonyms.
Basis: FTC Endorsement Guides and "Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers", FTC Act Section 5 (deceptive claims), FDA norms on drug/medical-device claims, Amazon Associates Program Operating Agreement. This list is for reference only and is not legal advice.

## Disclosure originals
| Program | Original |
|---|---|
| Amazon Associates (default) | As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. |
| Other affiliate programs | #ad This video contains affiliate links. I may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. |

Disclosure must be clear and conspicuous: in the video (start and near the end) and in the first line of the description, in plain words ("ad", "#ad", "commission"). Not enough on their own: "#sp", "#spon", "#collab", "#partner", "thanks [brand]", a disclosure only in a pinned comment or only behind "more".

## How to write the rewrite
- Efficacy → **feature / use scene**: "fights fatigue" ✗ → "Easy to flop down right after work" ○
- Superlative → **concrete fact**: "the best drying rack" ✗ → "Fits 10 towels at once" (confirmed number) ○
- Pressure → **check prompt**: "today only!" ✗ → "Check the current price at the link" ○
- Real-use claim → "Here's how it works"
